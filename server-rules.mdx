All players on our server are expected to follow these rules at all times. Staff members actively monitor the server, and violations are handled swiftly. Ignorance of the rules is not accepted as an excuse — read them before you play.

<Warning>
Repeated or severe rule violations will result in a **permanent ban** from the server. Staff decisions are final.
</Warning>

## General Conduct

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Rule 1 — Fail Roleplay (FRP)">
    All roleplay must maintain a realistic standard. Do not do anything that would be considered unrealistic in a real-world emergency response scenario. This includes unrealistic driving, impossible escapes, or ignoring in-game physics.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Rule 2 — Vehicle Deathmatch (VDM)">
    You may not use your vehicle as a weapon against other players without a valid, roleplay-grounded reason. Deliberately ramming or running over players is strictly prohibited.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Rule 3 — Random Deathmatch (RDM)">
    You may not shoot, attack, or kill another player without a valid roleplay reason. All use of force must be justified within the context of an active, believable scenario.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Rule 4 — Realistic Avatars">
    Your Roblox avatar must look realistic and resemble something you would see in real life. Body/head morphs, cartoon characters, and other non-realistic appearances are not allowed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Rule 5 — Display Names">
    If you use the in-game display name feature, you must create a realistic character name. Do not use inappropriate names or names that imply you are a staff member.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Roleplay Rules

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Rule 6 — Fear RP">
    Fear Roleplay is always active. If another player is pointing a weapon at you, you must comply with their demands. Acting as though you are not afraid of a gun pointed at you is a violation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Rule 7 — New Life Rule (NLR)">
    When your character dies or is arrested, your character has no memory of prior events in that life. You cannot return to the same scene or seek revenge. See the [New Life Rule](/roleplay/new-life-rule) page for full details.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Rule 8 — Cop Baiting">
    Deliberately trying to provoke law enforcement into a chase without a valid reason is not allowed. Do not start pointless pursuits simply because a police officer is nearby.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Rule 9 — Interrupting Roleplays">
    Do not interfere with another player's active roleplay scenario. If a scene is in progress, give it space.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Rule 10 — Robbery Spacing">
    You cannot rob multiple locations back-to-back. There is a **5-minute wait time** between robberies. Bank robberies require **prior staff approval**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Rule 11 — Group Limits">
    Criminal groups may not exceed **4 players**. For jewelry store robberies, the maximum group size is **3 players**. Do not use multiple vehicles to circumvent this rule.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Rule 12 — Priorities">
    While a priority event is active, you may not commit any criminal activity that would raise your wanted level. Wait for the priority to conclude.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Driving & Vehicle Rules

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Rule 13 — Realistic Driving">
    Drive realistically at all times. You must roleplay all crashes regardless of the speed involved.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Rule 14 — Yield to Emergency Vehicles">
    If an emergency vehicle is behind you with lights and sirens active, you must pull to the side and yield. Failure to do so is a violation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Rule 15 — Yield to Staff Cars">
    If a staff member is in a staff vehicle with lights active and pulls you over, you must stop immediately and comply.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Team-Specific Rules

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Rule 16 — Gun Motion">
    When pulling out any weapon other than a pistol or taser on a law enforcement officer, you must use gun motion (e.g., type "grabs gun" or "slings"). Gun motion is not required when drawing on civilians. Law enforcement do not need gun motion.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Rule 17 — SWAT Equipment">
    SWAT equipment — including the battering ram, Osiris Sniper, G36C, SWAT vest, Bearcat, and SWAT uniforms — may only be used by players who are trained and authorized in SWAT.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Rule 18 — Uniforms & Liveries">
    You must use the official uniforms and liveries for your department while in this server. Unauthorized uniforms or custom liveries that haven't been approved are not permitted.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Rule 19 — Safe Areas">
    You may not commit crimes inside the Fire Station, either Law Enforcement spawn points, or the DOT station. Violations in safe areas will be handled by staff immediately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Rule 20 — Detain Requests">
    You must always accept detain requests from law enforcement. If you believe the arrest is unjustified, comply and call for staff assistance afterward.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Rule 21 — Respawning">
    If you die, call for EMS and wait **2 minutes** before respawning if no EMS arrives. If there are no EMS players on the server, you may respawn immediately. If law enforcement was involved in your death, request a moderator to heal you so you can be taken into custody.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Voice Chat

<Note>
If the server session requires **Voice Chat (VC)**, you must have a working microphone and be present in the correct Discord voice channel before the session begins.
</Note>

## Enforcement

Rule violations are handled by our staff team. Depending on severity, consequences include:

| Action | When Applied |
|---|---|
| Verbal Warning | Minor first-time offences |
| Kill / Respawn | Resetting a rule-breaking scenario |
| Jail | Short-duration punishment (5 minutes) |
| Kick | Repeated minor offences or moderate violations |
| Temporary Ban | Serious violations or repeat offenders |
| Permanent Ban | Exploiting, severe violations, or extreme misconduct |

To report a rule violation, use `!mod` or `!help` in chat to request staff assistance, or see the [Reporting](/staff/reporting) page.
