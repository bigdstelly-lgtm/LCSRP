Our Discord server runs a custom bot that handles account verification, support, and a few community features. This page covers everything players can use.

## Linking Your Roblox Account

Before you can access most of the server, you need to link your Roblox account to Discord.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Run the Verify Command">
    Type `/verify` in any channel where the bot is present.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Roblox Username">
    Provide your exact Roblox username when prompted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get Verified">
    The bot looks up your Roblox account, sets your Discord nickname to match it, and gives you the verified role automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Use your exact Roblox username — the bot cannot verify a misspelled or partial name.
</Note>

## Getting Support (Tickets)

If you need to talk to staff privately, our ticket system creates a channel just for you and the team.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a Ticket">
    Click the **Open Ticket** button in the support channel. This creates a private channel only you and staff can see.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explain Your Issue">
    Describe what you need help with. A staff member will respond as soon as they're available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close When Finished">
    Once your issue is resolved, click **Close Ticket**. The channel is removed automatically a few seconds later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can only have one open ticket at a time. If you try to open another, the bot will point you to your existing one.
</Tip>

## Syncing Department Roles

If you're a member of one of our department servers (e.g. Police, Fire/EMS), you can sync your department roles into the main server automatically instead of waiting for staff to add them manually.

Use the role sync button/command provided in the departments channel. The bot checks your roles in each linked department server and grants the matching role here — if it can't find a match, make sure you actually hold the correct role in that department's own server first.

## Counting Game

We run a community counting game in a dedicated channel:

- Count up from 1, one number per message.
- You can't count twice in a row — let someone else go before you count again.
- Post the wrong number (or count out of turn) and the count resets to 1.
- Use `/counting-status` to see the current count and the server record.
- Use `/counting-leaderboard` to see who has counted correctly the most times.
