Our staff team is here to keep the server fair and enjoyable. If you witness a rule violation or find yourself in a situation that requires staff intervention, this page explains exactly how to get help.

## Calling for Staff In-Game

The quickest way to reach a staff member while in the server is through the in-game chat:

```
!mod
```
or
```
!help
```

Both commands send an **alert to all online moderators**, notifying them that a player needs assistance. A staff member will come to you as soon as possible.

<Tip>
When using `!mod` or `!help`, stay in your current location. Moving away from the scene makes it harder for staff to investigate.
</Tip>

## What to Say When Staff Arrives

When a staff member responds to your call:

1. **State the issue clearly** — "Player [username] RDM'd me near the bank"
2. **Provide the offending player's username** if possible
3. **Describe what happened** briefly and factually
4. **Avoid exaggerating** — staff investigate all reports impartially

Do not argue with other players while waiting for staff. Let the staff member mediate.

## Reporting Outside the Server

If the situation cannot be addressed in-game (e.g., you were banned before staff could respond, or the violating player left), report through our **Discord server**:

- Go to the moderation report channel
- Include the offending player's Roblox username
- Describe the incident with as much detail as possible
- Attach video or screenshot evidence if available

<Note>
Reports without evidence are still accepted, but evidence significantly speeds up the investigation process.
</Note>

## Appealing a Ban

If you believe you were banned unfairly, you can appeal through our Discord server:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Appeals Channel">
    Navigate to the ban appeals channel in our Discord server.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit Your Appeal">
    Provide your Roblox username, the reason you were told for the ban, and your explanation of what happened.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for a Review">
    A senior staff member will review your appeal. This may take 24–72 hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the Decision">
    Appeals are decided by senior staff. If your appeal is denied, the decision is final. Do not harass staff members over appeal outcomes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common Issues and What to Do

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Someone is RDM'ing or VDM'ing">
    Use `!mod` in chat immediately. Stay in a safe location and wait for a staff member. Do not retaliate — retaliating may get you in trouble too.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A player has an inappropriate avatar or name">
    Use `!mod` to call a staff member and describe the issue. Include the player's username.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I was wrongly kicked">
    If you were kicked by a staff member and believe it was incorrect, message us on Discord with your account name and the situation. Do not rejoin immediately and disrupt an ongoing investigation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Staff is not responding to !mod">
    There may not be any staff currently online. Check our Discord to see if staff are available, or post your report there for follow-up.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I witnessed a staff member abusing their powers">
    Report this directly to a Co-Owner or server Owner through Discord. Include as much detail as possible, including timestamps and any video evidence.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## What Staff Cannot Do

Our staff team is bound by the same community guidelines that govern all players. Staff may **not**:

- Ban players without a valid reason
- Use kill or teleport commands to grief players
- Share server codes or settings with unauthorized parties
- Discriminate against players based on team or personal preference

If a staff member violates these standards, report it immediately to server ownership.
