Our ER:LC private server supports five core teams, each offering a distinct roleplay experience. Before jumping in, choose the team that fits your playstyle and make sure you meet any requirements for restricted teams.

<Note>
Some teams may have **minimum player count requirements** or **Discord role restrictions** before they become available to you. Check the individual team pages for details.
</Note>

## Team Balance

Our server enforces **Team Balance** to prevent any one team from becoming overcrowded. If a team is full, you'll need to wait for a spot to open or choose a different team.

| Team | Typical Role |
|---|---|
| Police | Active patrol, traffic enforcement, felony arrests |
| Sheriff | County-level enforcement, pursuits, coordination |
| Fire | Structural and brush fires, vehicle extrications |
| EMS | Medical response, treating downed players |
| DOT | Road debris, broken traffic lights, infrastructure |
| Civilian | Everyday citizens, potential criminals, or neutral bystanders |

## Switching Teams

You can switch teams at any time by respawning and selecting a new team from the spawn menu. However:

- Do not switch teams mid-scene to escape an active roleplay
- Switching away from EMS while there are active injured players may be considered FRP
- Staff may instruct you to remain on your current team during a major event

<Tip>
If you're unsure which team to pick, start as a **Civilian**. It's the lowest-commitment role and lets you observe how the server operates before committing to a department.
</Tip>
