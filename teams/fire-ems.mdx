Fire and EMS players are the unsung heroes of Liberty County. As a firefighter, you battle structure fires, brush fires, and vehicle fires. As an EMS paramedic, you respond to injured players, provide treatment, and transport patients. Both roles require patience, communication, and the willingness to prioritise others' needs over personal action.

## Roles

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Fire Department" icon="fire-extinguisher">
    Respond to building, brush, and house fires. Perform vehicle extrications and provide scene safety support.
  </Card>
  <Card title="EMS" icon="heart-pulse">
    Treat injured and downed players. Coordinate with law enforcement at crime scenes and accident sites.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Fire Response

Fires can be triggered automatically by the server or manually started by a Moderator+ using staff commands. When a fire is active:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Receive the Call">
    Watch the in-game notification for the fire type (Building, Brush, or House) and location.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Respond to the Scene">
    Drive the fire apparatus to the location. Use lights and sirens. Civilians must yield.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Suppress the Fire">
    Equip your hose and begin suppression. Coordinate with your team to cover all sides of the fire.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear the Scene">
    Once the fire is out, ensure EMS clears any injured players before you leave. Mark the call complete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## EMS Response

EMS responds to injured or downed players across the server:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Receive the Request">
    Players who are downed should call for EMS. Watch for system notifications or `!mod` calls.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Respond to the Patient">
    Drive your EMS vehicle to the patient's location. Coordinate with Police if the scene is not yet secure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Treat the Patient">
    Use the EMS treatment tools to heal the injured player. Announce when the player is stable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Transport if Needed">
    For serious injuries, transport the patient to the hospital before clearing the call.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Important EMS Rules

<Warning>
Do not leave the server or switch teams while there are **active downed players** waiting for EMS. This may be treated as Fail Roleplay.
</Warning>

- Players must **call for EMS and wait 2 minutes** before respawning on their own — give EMS time to respond
- If law enforcement was involved, request a moderator to heal the player so they can be processed
- AI/NPC EMS calls may spawn automatically depending on server settings — these are separate from player-controlled EMS

## Automatic Events

Depending on server settings, fires and EMS calls may spawn automatically:

| Event | Automatic When Enabled |
|---|---|
| NPC EMS Calls | Random civilian injury calls appear on the map |
| Fires | Random fires spawn across Liberty County |
| DOT Debris Calls | Debris appears on roads for DOT to clear |
| Major Server Calls | Large-scale incidents trigger across the map |

<Tip>
If you're the only EMS or Fire player on the server, communicate in chat and ask staff or other players to assist. No single player is expected to handle all calls alone.
</Tip>
