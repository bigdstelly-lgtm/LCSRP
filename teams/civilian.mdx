Playing as a Civilian or DOT worker gives you the most freedom of any role on the server. Civilians can explore Liberty County, interact with other players, run errands, and even engage in criminal activity — all within the server's rules. DOT workers respond to road hazards, debris, and traffic infrastructure issues.

## Civilian Role

As a civilian, you can:

- Drive freely across Liberty County
- Interact with law enforcement, EMS, and other civilians in character
- Engage in legal activities like delivery jobs, exploration, and socialising
- Engage in **legal, rule-compliant criminal activity** (robberies, evasion) following all applicable rules
- Roleplay as a unique character with a display name

### What You Cannot Do as a Civilian

- Exploit game mechanics or commit VDM/RDM
- Commit crimes during an active **priority event**
- Rob more than one location within a **5-minute window**
- Rob the bank without **prior staff approval**
- Carry restricted weapons without in-character justification
- Commit crimes inside **safe zones** (Fire Station, LEO spawns, DOT station)

## Department of Transportation (DOT)

DOT workers maintain Liberty County's road infrastructure:

| Responsibility | Details |
|---|---|
| Debris Calls | Respond to road debris spawned automatically or via staff |
| Broken Traffic Lights | Repair traffic lights that malfunction during play |
| Road Hazard Management | Clear obstructions and keep traffic flowing |

<Note>
DOT debris calls can be toggled on or off by the server owner. When enabled, calls will appear on your map just like fire or EMS calls.
</Note>

## Criminal Activity Rules

If you choose to play as a criminal, these rules govern your actions:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Group Size Limits">
    Criminal groups may not exceed **4 players** for general crime. Jewelry store robberies are limited to **3 players**. Do not use multiple vehicles to work around this rule.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Robbery Cooldown">
    You must wait **5 minutes** between robberies. Spree robbing is not allowed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Bank Robbery">
    You must request **staff permission** before robbing the bank. Do not start a bank robbery without approval.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Initiating Pursuits">
    You must have a valid reason to flee from police. Do not start pointless pursuits just because a cop is nearby.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Fear RP">
    Fear RP always applies to you. If a weapon is pointed at you, comply with demands.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Display Names

You are encouraged to use a **character name** as your display name to enhance immersion:

- Choose a realistic first and last name
- Do not use names that are inappropriate or that claim staff status
- You will be kicked for using an inappropriate roleplay name

<Tip>
A well-crafted civilian character with a backstory makes every interaction more engaging — both for you and the officers or emergency responders you encounter.
</Tip>
