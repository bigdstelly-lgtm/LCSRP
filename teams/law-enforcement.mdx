Law enforcement is the backbone of any ER:LC roleplay session. As a Police officer or Sheriff's deputy, you are responsible for patrolling Liberty County, responding to calls, conducting traffic stops, and apprehending wanted players. This role comes with significant responsibility — and significant authority.

## Roles

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Police" icon="car-burst">
    City-based patrol officers. Handle traffic enforcement, pursuits, and most front-line criminal response.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sheriff" icon="star">

  </Card>

## Duties

As a law enforcement officer on our server, your primary duties include:

- **Patrol** designated areas and respond to dispatch calls
- **Conduct traffic stops** for violations (speeding, red light infractions, reckless driving)
- **Respond to crimes in progress** — robberies, pursuits, and armed incidents
- **Coordinate with EMS** when players are injured during incidents
- **Apprehend wanted players** using arrests, not excessive force

## Equipment & Uniforms

<Warning>
You must wear the **official law enforcement uniform and livery** approved for our server at all times. Unauthorized uniforms are a rule violation.
</Warning>

| Equipment | Notes |
|---|---|
| Standard sidearm | Available to all LEO |
| Taser | Available to all LEO — no gun motion required |
| Rifles / Shotguns | Available based on scenario |
| SWAT gear (Bearcat, G36C, etc.) | **Authorized SWAT members only** |

## Use of Force

All use of force must be justified within the roleplay context:

- You must have a **valid reason** before drawing or using your weapon
- Deadly force is a last resort — always attempt de-escalation and less-lethal options first
- You do **not** need to use gun motion when drawing your weapon
- Shooting an unarmed, compliant suspect is an RDM violation

## Arrests & Detains

- All players are required to **accept detain requests** under server rules
- If a player claims your arrest is unjustified, they must comply first and dispute it with staff afterward
- Do not arrest players without a valid reason — this is considered power abuse and may result in your own demotion or kick

## Pursuits

- You must have a **valid reason** to initiate a pursuit (observed crime, felony stop, active warrant)
- Do not initiate pursuits purely for fun or to harass civilians
- Keep communication on team channels during active pursuits
- Coordinate a roadblock or spike strips where appropriate

<Tip>
During a **priority event**, all available units should respond to assist. Non-essential patrols should hold position or redirect unless specifically needed.
</Tip>
