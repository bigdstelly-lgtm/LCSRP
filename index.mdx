## How This Server Works

Our server is a **private, structured ER:LC roleplay experience** built around realistic emergency response scenarios. Players take on roles as law enforcement officers, firefighters, paramedics, DOT workers, or civilians, and are expected to behave in accordance with real-world standards throughout their session.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Join the Server">
    Use the server join code or link shared in our Discord to enter the server. New players join a public queue and are admitted as spots open up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick Your Team">
    Select from Law Enforcement, Fire & EMS, DOT, or Civilian. Some teams have requirements — check the Teams section before switching.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the Rules">
    Familiarise yourself with the server rules before your first roleplay. Violations are handled by our staff team and can result in kicks or bans.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Roleplaying">
    Respond to calls, conduct patrols, or play as a civilian. Follow the roleplay guidelines, respect other players, and have fun.
  </Step>
</Steps>
