Our Emergency Response: Liberty County (ER:LC) private server is a structured, community-run roleplay environment built on the Roblox game ER:LC. Unlike public servers, our private server enforces realistic roleplay standards, uses a staff moderation team, and offers a focused experience for players who want immersive emergency response scenarios.

## What Is ER:LC?

Emergency Response: Liberty County is a Roblox game developed by **Police Roleplay Community (PRC)** where players take on roles as law enforcement, firefighters, paramedics, DOT workers, or civilians in the fictional Liberty County. Players respond to calls, patrol the streets, fight fires, and interact in a living open world.

## Important: Player Data

<Warning>
Player data — including cash, XP, and purchased items — is **not saved** on private servers. Any progress you make on our server will not carry over to public servers, and vice versa.
</Warning>
