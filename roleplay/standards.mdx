Our private server holds every player to a consistent standard of roleplay quality. These standards exist to keep sessions immersive, fair, and enjoyable for everyone — from the civilian on the street to the senior officer on patrol. This page outlines what good roleplay looks like and the common mistakes to avoid.

## The Core Standard: Realism

All roleplay on our server must maintain a **realistic standard**. Ask yourself: "Would this happen in real life?" If the answer is no, it's probably Fail Roleplay (FRP).

Good roleplay looks like:
- Responding to situations as a real person would
- Using realistic driving speeds for the situation
- Complying with law enforcement when appropriate (see Fear RP)
- Treating injuries seriously and waiting for EMS
- Communicating in character during active scenes

## Common Roleplay Violations

| Violation | Description |
|---|---|
| Fail Roleplay (FRP) | Acting unrealistically — ignoring injuries, impossible escapes, breaking immersion |
| Metagaming | Using out-of-character information (e.g., a Discord message) to gain in-game advantage |
| Powergaming | Forcing outcomes on other players without giving them a chance to respond |
| Cop Baiting | Deliberately provoking law enforcement without a roleplay reason |
| Interrupting Scenes | Walking into or disrupting another player's active roleplay |

## Voice Chat (VC) Sessions

Some sessions require players to be in a Discord voice channel. When VC is required:

- You must have a **working microphone** ready before loading in
- Join the correct Discord VC **before** spawning in the game
- Remain in the VC for the duration of the session
- Keep background noise to a minimum

## Safe Areas

These locations are **safe zones** where criminal activity is strictly prohibited:

- Fire Station
- Law Enforcement spawn points (both)
- DOT Station

<Warning>
Committing crimes in safe areas will result in immediate staff intervention, including removal from the server.
</Warning>

## Interacting With Staff

When a staff member speaks to you:

- Respond promptly and honestly
- Do not flee or disconnect — this is considered an admission of guilt
- If you disagree with a staff decision, raise it calmly after the situation is resolved
- Use `!mod` or `!help` in chat to request staff assistance when needed
