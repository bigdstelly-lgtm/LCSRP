Combat is a natural part of ER:LC roleplay, but it must be grounded in a realistic, justified context. Our server enforces strict rules around violence to prevent griefing and ensure that every confrontation is part of a meaningful scenario. This page covers everything you need to know about when and how combat is permitted.

## Random Deathmatch (RDM)

RDM is shooting, attacking, or killing another player **without a valid roleplay reason**. It is one of the most common violations and is taken very seriously.

**Examples of RDM:**
- Shooting a player because you're bored
- Killing a player because they bumped into your car
- Opening fire on a police officer without a prior confrontation

**NOT RDM (valid reasons):**
- Defending yourself when a weapon is pointed at you
- Shooting at officers during an active armed standoff you initiated
- Responding to an attack on yourself or a confirmed allied player

## Vehicle Deathmatch (VDM)

VDM is using your vehicle as a weapon to injure or kill players **without justification**. Ramming, running over, or pinning players with a vehicle is prohibited unless there is a clear, in-character reason.

**Examples of VDM:**
- Intentionally running over a pedestrian for fun
- Ramming a police cruiser to escape a stop with no prior confrontation
- Using your car to knock a player off a ledge

## Fail Roleplay (FRP) in Combat

Combat-related FRP includes any action that breaks realism during a violent encounter:

| Example | Why It's FRP |
|---|---|
| Ignoring bullet wounds and running away normally | Real people don't shrug off gunshots |
| Jumping off a building to escape police | Unrealistic escape method |
| Immediately re-engaging after being downed | Contradicts the New Life Rule |
| Refusing to comply when a gun is pointed at you | Violates Fear RP |

## Fear Roleplay

Fear RP is **always active** on our server. If another player has a firearm aimed at you, you must:

- Stop what you're doing
- Comply with their demands
- React as a real person would — with fear

Ignoring a weapon pointed directly at you is a Fear RP violation, even if you think you can escape.

## Gun Motion

When drawing a weapon **on a law enforcement officer**, you must announce gun motion in chat (except for pistols and tasers):

```
*grabs AR from waistband*
*slings rifle*
*draws shotgun*
```

<Note>
Gun motion is only required when drawing on **law enforcement**. You do not need gun motion when drawing on civilians. Law enforcement players never need gun motion.
</Note>

| Item | Restriction |
|---|---|
| Bearcat (armoured vehicle) | SWAT only |
| G36C | SWAT only |
| Osiris Sniper | SWAT only |
| Battering Ram | SWAT only |
| SWAT Vest | SWAT only |
| SWAT Uniforms | SWAT only |

Using SWAT equipment without authorization is a rule violation and will result in a kick.

## Reporting Combat Violations

If you witness RDM, VDM, or other combat violations:

1. Type `!mod` or `!help` in chat to call a staff member
2. Provide the offending player's username
3. Describe what happened clearly and calmly
4. Do not take matters into your own hands — let staff handle it
