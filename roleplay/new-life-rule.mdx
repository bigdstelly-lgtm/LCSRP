The **New Life Rule (NLR)** is one of the fundamental pillars of structured roleplay. It governs what happens to your character after they die or are arrested — and it exists to prevent players from using out-of-character knowledge to affect ongoing scenes they should no longer be part of.

## What Is the New Life Rule?

When your character **dies or is arrested**, that chapter of their life ends. Your new character spawning in has:

- **No memory** of the previous life's events
- **No grudge** against the players who killed or arrested you
- **No knowledge** of the scene you just left

This means you cannot return to the same area, re-engage the same players, or continue a scenario that ended with your character's death.

## NLR in Practice

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="After Your Character Dies">
    - Do not return to the location where you died for the duration of the active scene
    - Do not seek out the players who killed you for revenge
    - Treat your new spawn as a fresh start — no memory of prior events
    - If law enforcement was involved, call for a staff member to process you rather than simply respawning
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="After Your Character Is Arrested">
    - Accept the arrest and roleplay through the custody process
    - Do not escape custody using unrealistic methods
    - Once released or processed, your character's previous criminal activity is resolved — do not resume the same scenario
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="After Being Downed (Not Dead)">
    - Call for EMS and wait **2 minutes** before respawning if no EMS responds
    - If EMS is present, wait for treatment before respawning
    - If law enforcement is involved, wait for a moderator to heal you so you can be taken into custody properly
    - Do not respawn early to avoid the consequences of the scene
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Common NLR Violations

| Violation | Why It Breaks NLR |
|---|---|
| Returning to your death location mid-scene | Your character doesn't know where they died |
| Resuming a pursuit after respawning | The chase ended when you died |
| Immediately attacking the officer who arrested you | Revenge is not realistic after arrest/death |
| Telling friends in Discord where enemies are after dying | Using post-death information in-game (metagaming) |

## Respawning Rules

<Steps>
  <Step title="You Are Downed">
    Type in chat that you are downed and call for EMS. Wait for a response.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait 2 Minutes">
    If no EMS responds within 2 minutes, you may respawn on your own. If EMS is present, wait for treatment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="If Police Were Involved">
    Request a **moderator** to heal you so you can be processed into custody correctly. Do not respawn to avoid arrest.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Respawn and Start Fresh">
    When you respawn, your character has no memory of the previous life. Begin a new scenario.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Respawning early to avoid arrest or escape an active scene is a Fail Roleplay violation. Staff will take action if they observe this.
</Warning>

## Why NLR Matters

NLR keeps roleplay fair and continuous. Without it, every death becomes meaningless — players simply respawn and pick up exactly where they left off, turning combat into a grind rather than a consequence-driven story. Respecting NLR makes every scene more tense, more meaningful, and more rewarding for everyone involved.
